What goes into the model?

Pickup, delivery, distance, equipment, weight, and date. The model also derives lane, calendar, and location features. Missing or invalid weights use equipment medians learned from the training fold. The unstable market and quote signals were excluded after a future-month comparison.

What should I keep in mind?

There are ten labeled months from one year. This curve cannot establish December holiday peaks or future market shocks. The $100.26 historical error is not a confidence interval for an individual load. Hidden November–December accuracy is unknown.
